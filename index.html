About Sakura Sanctuary

Procedural 3D Environment

Sakura Sanctuary is an immersive real-time 3D experience powered by Three.js. It features a procedurally generated cherry blossom tree with thousands of floating petals, dynamic golden hour lighting, soft shadows, and a serene ambient soundscape.

Click and drag to rotate the camera view
Scroll to zoom in and out of the sanctuary
Use bottom controls to switch atmospheric lighting
